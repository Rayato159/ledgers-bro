# Update 0.1.15

Recurring installments now distinguish **Repay existing credit card debt** from **New expense / new card purchase**. A plan assigned to a card opens in repayment mode, with the card selected and the funding account left for you to choose. Select the bank or cash account used to pay, check the receiving card, amount and date, then confirm.

A repayment decreases the funding balance and card debt by the same amount. It does not create another expense. The installment becomes paid atomically with the transfer. New card charges remain available under the explicit new-expense option. Existing transfers can be linked without moving money again. Cancelling a linked repayment restores both balances and reopens its installment.

The payment review names both accounts. New repayments exceeding recorded debt are rejected, including when the debt changes between review and confirmation. Keep the app updated on devices that open backups containing transfer-linked installments; older versions only understood expense-linked installments.

The new tanuki salaryman mascot follows Lookhin's original illustration references. The full-body artwork appears in existing illustration areas; a compact face mark is used in navigation and launcher icons. Masters and generation prompts are under `design/tanuki/`.

Windows MSI/ZIP and Android ARM64/x86_64 test APKs are supplied through GitHub Releases. Local AI models remain separate downloads. Existing ledgers are retained; the update does not automatically reclassify historical card charges.
