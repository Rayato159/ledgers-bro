# Generation prompt set

Tool: built-in `image_gen`. Each asset used a separate call. Lumi's modern-fantasy portrait was then the style reference for the five other characters. These briefs document the final creative instructions; the previous black-haired-character and sailor-outfit iterations were rejected and are not shipped.

## Shared requirements

Create one new original kawaii anime chibi young-adult character for a personal-finance app, with appealing otaku anime character design. Beautiful hand-drawn pencil/digital ink outlines, subtle grain and soft pastel watercolor/cel shading. Modern Fantasy urban fashion: charcoal streetwear jackets, ivory high-neck layers, short asymmetric capes, silver geometric or constellation details, and practical small shoes. Modest outfits, no school uniforms, no gold, no animals, no existing franchise character. Correct anatomy and natural hands; two arms, five fingers where visible, sensible feet and standing waist folds. Isolated true-alpha transparent background, complete silhouette inside square framing with generous margin. No text, numbers, watermark, border or scenery. Retain charcoal and natural skin colors without applying the UI's accent as a color cast.

## Lumi — logo, login and overview

New original young-adult chibi girl with fluffy dusty-lavender shoulder-length hair, an asymmetrical side braid, a pale-blue star clip, large sparkling blue-gray eyes, a gentle smile and blush. Half-body portrait holding a lavender enchanted ledger notebook against her chest. Replace the earlier sailor cardigan and bow with an oversized cropped charcoal techwear jacket, high ivory collar, lilac lining, a short shoulder cape, silver crescent clasp and subtle constellation stitching, ivory ribbed top and crossbody spellbook strap. Strong readable icon silhouette. Preserve the new character's face, hair and grainy illustration style.
