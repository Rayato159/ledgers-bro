# Tanuki artwork prompts

Tool: built-in image generation. Both assets requested transparent backgrounds.

## Mascot

Use the owner's hand-drawn scene and character references for style. Replace the human subject with an adult anthropomorphic tanuki Japanese salaryman: squat stocky body, round ears, brown fur, cream muzzle, dark eye mask, short doglike muzzle and plain bushy tail. Half-lidded tired mischievous eyes, asymmetric brow and crooked grin. Oversized charcoal suit with dropped shoulders, baggy trousers, loose pale shirt, loosened tie and worn office shoes. One paw in a pocket and the other holding a small lit cigarette with a restrained smoke curl. Heavy imperfect charcoal contours, muted flat cel colors, sketchy folds and subtle interior grain. Full body centered with safe margins. No text, scenery, weapons, extra props, glossy 3D or corporate vector styling.

Refinement: preserve the character, pose and colors; extract the background and remove outer glows, shadows and atmospheric shading, retaining the complete silhouette and smoke on alpha.

## Logo

Derive a compact app mark from the exact mascot. Large centered head, small suit collar and loose tie; retain round ears, cheek tufts, tired eyes and crooked smile. A small cigarette at one corner of the mouth, without hands or a smoke cloud. Simplify small details for a 32-pixel icon. Thick imperfect ink and muted cel colors; nearly square silhouette with clear margins, transparent background, no badge, lettering, backdrop or shadow.
